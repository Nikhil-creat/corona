# Electrical Safety Compliance Log Extract

An electrical fault, such as arcing or abnormal draw, requires the node to be isolated from the supply with a grace period of at most 15 seconds. Isolate is mandatory and overrides throttle load. Inspection must follow within 24 hours.

Compliance requirement: every remediation affecting energised equipment is recorded with approver, nonce and verification result in the audit trail. Emergency shutdown is permitted only when isolation is not possible.
