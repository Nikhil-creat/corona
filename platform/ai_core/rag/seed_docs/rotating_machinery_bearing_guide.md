# Rotating Machinery Bearing Wear Guide

Bearing wear is indicated by rising vibration above 6 millimetres per second RMS, often with optical evidence of lubricant leakage. For moderate wear an inspection must be scheduled within a window of 1 to 168 hours, shorter windows for higher severity.

When severity is high, above 0.7, the machine must be isolated from the process line. To isolate a node, remove it from active routing with a grace period of 0 to 300 seconds. Isolation is a physical intervention and needs human approval.

Repeated bearing wear events within 90 days indicate systemic lubrication failure and must be escalated to the reliability engineer.
