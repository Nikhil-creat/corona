# Structural Integrity and Crack Response Procedure

Structural stress above 70 percent of rated capacity combined with an optical crack detection is a structural anomaly. Any confirmed structural crack requires that the affected node be isolated immediately, with a grace period not exceeding 30 seconds, and an inspection within 12 hours.

Isolate actions in a structural context are physical interventions. They must be approved by an authorised engineer using a signed decision. Do not throttle load on a cracked structure because cyclic loading propagates the crack.

Regulatory note: all structural isolations must be logged for compliance audit with the approver identity and timestamp.
